About me

Engineer by training, builder by habit

I'm Krrish, a Mechanical Engineering and Management student at McMaster University. I grew up in Calgary and moved to Hamilton for school.

The short version

I like projects where I have to do the whole thing: draw it, print it, wire it, code it, then find out what I got wrong. The rover on this site is the clearest example. The first frame bent under load, and fixing that taught me more about materials than any lecture did.

The management half of my degree is not a side note. I enjoy financial models and consulting cases as much as CAD, and I think the best engineers understand why something is worth building, not just how.

Outside class

This summer I did my first co-op as a mechanical engineering intern at ROZOR, designing chassis parts for an autonomous delivery and disinfection robot. During the term I'm a mechanical engineer on the McMaster Aerial Robotics & Drones team and on IEEE McMaster's Bionic Arm team. I've also been an operations engineer with Google Developer Groups McMaster and a part-time tutor with Varsity Tutors.

I chose engineering because it lets me follow what I love most: turning conceptual ideas into real objects that drive change. Outside of that, I'm in the gym, on a game with friends, or printing something new. More on the Fun page.
