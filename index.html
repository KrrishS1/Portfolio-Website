About

Engineer by training, builder by habit

I study Mechanical Engineering and Management (Co-op) at McMaster. I grew up in Calgary and moved to Hamilton for school.

This summer I spent my first co-op term at ROZOR, packaging the chassis of an autonomous delivery and disinfection robot in SolidWorks: GD&T drawings, tolerance stack-ups and FEA, checked by hand. During the term I design airframes and payload mechanisms for McMaster Aerial Robotics and Drones and the hand and wrist of IEEE McMaster's bionic arm. More on my clubs and design teams.

The management half of my degree shows up too. My team of three won the consulting track at the McMaster Engineering Competition 2026 with a safety standard for space tourism, budget and risk model included.

How I work

  • Build in layers. Get a working version before adding the next feature.
  • Check the model. Every FEA result gets a hand calculation next to it.
  • Design for the failure. Know where a part will bend before it does.
  • Write it down. A failure that isn't documented will happen again.
