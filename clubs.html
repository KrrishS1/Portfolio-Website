McMaster Aerial Robotics and Drones

Mechanical Engineer, Airframe and Payload

A 50-member student team that designs, builds and flies drones.

  • Design and prototype a servo-actuated release mechanism rated to 3 kg, with tool-free mounts so the payload bay reconfigures between missions.
  • Lead structural design of the plate-frame UAV chassis, optimizing load path and stiffness to weight for plate fabrication.
  • Bench-test proof-of-principle assemblies and run root cause analysis on recurring failures using flight and bench test data.
  • Wrote the assembly, test and verification procedures the whole team builds to, and settle mounting tolerance and vibration issues with the electrical and software leads.

IEEE McMaster Bionic Arm

Subteam Member, Mechanical Design and Integration

An IEEE EMBS student team building a robotic hand and arm that grips objects on command, the first step toward camera-guided picking and a humanoid robot.

  • Design the tendon-driven fingers, palm and wrist in CAD.
  • Select and fit the motors, gears and cable routing that pull each finger like a tendon, so the actuators sit in the arm and the hand stays light and compact.
  • Design the dual-mode mount that lets one arm switch between a prosthetic worn by a person and a robot fixed to a base.
  • Iterate from CAD to 3D-printed prototypes and back, testing each part's fit and motion before full assembly.
